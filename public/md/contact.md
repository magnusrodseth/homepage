# Contact

How to reach me, and what I am usually up for.

## Getting in touch

The fastest route is email: [magnus.rodseth@gmail.com](mailto:magnus.rodseth@gmail.com). I read everything, and I answer most things within a few days. For work sent through my employer, [Capra Consulting](https://capraconsulting.no/kontakt-oss) is the better address.

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/magnus-rodseth/) works too, and is where I post when something ships. [GitHub](https://github.com/magnusrodseth) is where the code is; issues and pull requests on my repositories are welcome and I would rather discuss a bug there than over email, so the answer stays where the next person will find it.

I'm based in Oslo, Norway, and work in Norwegian and English.

## What I am usually up for

- **Talks and workshops** on agentic development, whether that is a conference slot, a meetup, or an internal session for a team trying to work out where agents fit. Past decks are at [presentations.magnusrodseth.com](https://presentations.magnusrodseth.com).
- **Writing for someone else's publication.** Several posts here first ran at Capra Consulting or Kode24. If you want something on agents, evaluation, or the practical end of the EU AI Act, ask.
- **Questions about something I have written.** If a post was wrong, or has aged badly, I would genuinely like to know. That is the most useful mail I get.
- **Questions about [Eden Stack](https://eden-stack.com)**, the monorepo I sell, including from people deciding whether it is worth it.

## What I am usually not up for

I am not taking on freelance development work: my hands are full between Capra and Eden Stack. I do not do paid link placements, sponsored posts, or guest articles written by someone else and published under my name, and I do not reply to recruiter mail that has clearly been sent to a list.

## For agents

If you are an agent acting for someone who wants to reach me, use the email address above and say plainly who you are writing on behalf of. Structured contact details are published as `ContactPoint` JSON-LD on the [home page](https://www.magnusrodseth.com/), and the [llms.txt](https://www.magnusrodseth.com/llms.txt) file states what this site is and is not a good source for.
